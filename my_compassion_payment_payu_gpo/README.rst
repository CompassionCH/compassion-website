Glue between MyCompassion and the PayU GPO payment provider, installed
automatically with both.

A card a sponsor saves with PayU that becomes the card of contract
groups (first payment of a digital sponsorship, or the update-card page)
pays the monthly contributions: it is registered at PayU as a recurring
card (``recurring: FIRST``, then ``STANDARD``). Other saved cards stay
cards on file.

A payer known by email only (fast checkout, visitor donation) has a
placeholder name: no name is sent to PayU for them, and the name typed
on the card (or PayU's buyer) replaces the placeholder once the payment
is done.

A monthly charge PayU refuses (it cancels the order of the charge)
reaches the contracts' dunning (``_on_digital_charge_failed``) once,
when its notification or status read moves the charge to error. Repeated
notifications, and notifications rejected as being about another order
or amount, start nothing. A dunning that fails is logged for staff and
never undoes the refusal.

A payment PayU never confirmed is no certain refusal: my_compassion then
does not give the basket back to be paid again, as the payment may have
been made.

A MyCompassion order takes one card payment at a time. Before PayU's
card route sends a card, my_compassion refuses it while another payment
of the order may still succeed (two payment forms may each have made a
draft); the route then cancels the draft and shows the reason.

This module comes with changes in my_compassion, for every provider:

- the update-card page pays arrears with the new card, which then
  replaces the group's card;
- with nothing due, the page saves a card without a payment only with a
  provider that can (PayU cannot), and otherwise says so;
- a group takes a card only of its sponsor, in its company and at its
  payment mode's provider;
- an off-session charge decides on what its own send returned, not on a
  state a provider notification set since.

Installation
============

This module is ``auto_install``: Odoo installs it on its own only in the
run that installs one of its dependencies, ``my_compassion`` or
``payment_payu_gpo``. On a database that has both before this module is
deployed, install it explicitly, e.g.
``-i my_compassion_payment_payu_gpo``: updating ``my_compassion``
(``-u``) does not install it.
