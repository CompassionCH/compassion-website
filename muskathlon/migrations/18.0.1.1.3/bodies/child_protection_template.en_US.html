At Compassion, we believe that children deserve to be valued, loved and protected. That is why love, care and protection of children come first for us. As children living in extreme poverty are among the most vulnerable, Compassion has drawn up clear guidelines to guarantee the safety and protection of every child we support.

Everyone who comes into direct contact with the children must therefore provide a current criminal record certificate. It is the last document we still need:

Send my criminal record certificate

Please also read and sign the child protection declaration.

You can find more detailed information here: https://compassion.ch/de/kinderschutz/
